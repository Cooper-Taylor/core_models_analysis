# ΔG′° source scatter plots, run subtractively

The mirror image of `../thermo_source_dg_scatter_filtered/`. There, at each
threshold T, the reactions both sources are confident about are **kept** and
everything else is thrown away. Here those same reactions are **removed** and
what is left over is drawn.

Generated by `scripts/plot_thermo_source_dg_scatter_subtractive.py` (no
arguments, ~9 min; `--no-singles` for just the grids in ~4 min; `--cache` parks
the parsed source table so re-runs skip the ~2.5-min reload). The loader, the
panel renderer, both ellipses and the palette are imported from the additive
script, so the two families of figures cannot drift apart — only the mask
differs.

Same data as the additive figures: **ModelSEED `dev` @ 49563c6f**, snapshotted to
`/scratch/ctaylor/tmp/devsnap2` (Group Contribution rebuilt under Convention A);
ê from `results/eq_vs_dgpms_gcA/`.

## Two removal rules

| rule | remove reaction iff | survivors are |
|---|---|---|
| **`any`** | **both** sources ≤ T | reactions at least one source is unsure about — the **exact complement** of the additive panel at T |
| **`both`** | **either** source ≤ T | reactions **no** source vouches for — one confident source is enough to retire a reaction |

`any` is the literal subtraction: n kept here + n kept in the additive panel at
the same T = the pair's base set, exactly, in all 36 slices. `both` is the
strict residue, and it empties out far faster.

`filtered` draws only the survivors and rescales the axes to them; `context`
draws everything with the removed reactions grayed out and axes shared per row.
Thresholds T ∈ {0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20} kcal/mol, criteria σ and ê as in the
additive README.

## NaN criterion counts as "more than T"

ê is NaN where the assignment model refuses a source (dGPredictor-ModelSEED on
quinone/quinol; eQuilibrator sentinels are already dropped at load). In the
additive figures a NaN fails the filter and the reaction is excluded. Here a
refused source is treated as the opposite of a confident one, so it never counts
toward removal: under `any` the reaction is unremovable, under `both` its finite
partner can still retire it. That is what makes `any` an exact complement. The
count rides in `n_nan_crit` and in each single panel's footnote — it matters,
because at ê ≥ 10 the GC∩dGPMS survivors are **486 refusals out of 4,601**, and
at ê ≥ 20, **486 of 873**.

## What the subtraction shows

**1. The confident subsets are small, so `any` barely changes the picture — for
σ.** Removing everything at σ ≤ 20 leaves 9% of GC∩eQ, and that residue still
reads r = 0.85, median \|Δ\| = **2.23** against a pooled 2.62. Its agreement is
*better* than the full set's, after deleting every reaction either source
claimed to be sure of. The σ ordering is informative at the very bottom (the
additive panels get 0.88 at σ ≤ 1) but it does not order the rest of the pair:
GC and eQuilibrator agree about as well on the reactions they both distrust.

**2. Where σ is informative, subtraction is where you see it monotonically.**
GC vs dGPredictor-ModelSEED degrades cleanly as the confident reactions are
removed — median \|Δ\| 6.43 → 6.77 → 7.34 → 8.34 → 9.15 → **13.10** across
T = 0.5 … 20, r 0.804 → 0.769. eQuilibrator vs dGPredictor-ModelSEED goes
3.74 → **10.41**. Read together with the additive figures (that pair reaches
r = 0.99 at σ ≤ 1), the two directions agree: σ genuinely ranks those pairs.

**3. ê ranks the data much better than σ, and only the subtractive view shows
it.** Additively, ê ≤ T panels all look excellent (r 0.95–0.998), which proves
only that the confident set is good. Subtractively the residue falls apart the
way it should: GC vs dGPMS after removing ê ≤ 10 keeps 4,601 reactions at
r = 0.67 and median \|Δ\| = **23.4**; after ê ≤ 20, 873 reactions at r = 0.47 and
**65.1**. eQ vs dGPMS after ê ≤ 20 keeps 646 at r = **0.24** and 58.9. σ never
produces anything like that separation — its worst `any` residue is GC∩dGPMS at
13.1. **ê is the criterion to filter on; σ is a weak proxy for it.**

**4. ê_GC is nearly a constant, and that is a real limit.** Across GC∩eQ and
GC∩dGPMS, ê_GC spans only **3.04 to 5.70** kcal/mol. It cannot mark a Group
Contribution reaction as trustworthy (the additive ê ≤ 2 panels are empty) and
it cannot mark one as hopeless either — so every GC panel here empties the
moment T clears 5.70 (`both`) or the partner's own ceiling (`any`). The ceilings,
i.e. the smallest T that empties a panel: GC vs eQ σ 566.6 / ê 7.86 under `any`,
65.4 / 5.70 under `both`; GC vs dGPMS σ 1960.9 / ê ∞ (the refusals) under `any`,
566.6 / 5.70 under `both`; eQ vs dGPMS σ 1111.2 / ê ∞ under `any`, 64.3 / 7.86
under `both`. Each empty single panel prints its own value.

**5. The ΔG′° = 0-in-both cluster is exactly the confident set.** The additive
README's trap — 95% of GC∩dGPMS survivors at σ ≤ 1 are zero in both sources —
inverts here: that pair's zero-in-both share falls 12.1% (pooled) → 0.8%
(`any`, σ ≤ 20) → **0.10%** (`both`, σ ≤ 1). Subtracting the confident reactions
subtracts the free agreement with them, which is most of why the residue's
median \|Δ\| rises.

**6. The `both` rule bottoms out in genuine nonsense.** GC∩eQ keeps 10,904
reactions at σ > 0.5 in both sources, 814 at σ > 2, and **4** at σ > 20 —
rxn31420, rxn31514, rxn31540, rxn31542, all malonyl-CoA / cinnamoyl-CoA
polyketide-type condensations. On those four GC reports +106 to +379 kcal/mol
and eQuilibrator −224 to −551, with σ of 296–567 and 30–65 respectively: r =
−0.58, median \|Δ\| = 648 kcal/mol. Both sources are loudly announcing that they
cannot do these, and both are right. eQ∩dGPMS narrows the same way, to 3
reactions at σ > 20 (median \|Δ\| 1,269).

## Base set

Identical to the additive figures, and computed by the same `base_set()`:
reactions where both sources have a non-sentinel ΔG′° and a defined (non-`?`)
stored operator; eQuilibrator sentinels (σ > 100, 4,934 values) dropped at load;
\|ΔG′°\| > 1500 kcal/mol dropped (9 / 19 / 11 per pair). Sizes: GC∩EQ **18,157**,
GC∩DGPMS **25,950**, EQ∩DGPMS **19,731**.

Point color is the reversibility transition between the two sources.

## The two ellipses

Defined and justified in the additive README — the same two, computed over the
**survivors** here:

- **filled** = 95% concentration ellipse. Covariance shape at the empirical 95th
  percentile of Mahalanobis $d^2$, so coverage is distribution-free and the
  legend prints the realised fraction. Major axis = the total-least-squares
  (orthogonal) fit, tilt readable against 45°; semi-minor = the half-width
  holding 95% of the reactions. This is the one to quote about the cloud.
- **dashed** = minimum-volume enclosing (Löwner–John) ellipse: unique minimiser
  of $-\log\det A$ s.t. $(z_i-c)^\top A(z_i-c)\le 1$, fixed by ≤ 5 points, with
  $\tfrac12 E \subseteq \mathrm{conv}(P) \subseteq E$. A convex-hull proxy and an
  extremes statistic — breakdown point zero — not a description of the cloud.

The subtraction makes the gap between them vivid: removing the confident
reactions leaves the *outliers* untouched, so the dashed ellipse barely moves
while the filled one grows. Both sets of numbers are in
`slice_counts_subtractive.tsv` (`conc_*`, `mvee_*`).
