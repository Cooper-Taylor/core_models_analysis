What this is

For every core model, each reaction was set — one at a time, from the model's default — to < reverse, > forward, = reversible, and ? off/knockout, re-solving biomass growth each time. A reaction's direction-sensitivity for a model is the spread of growth across those four options; knockout-essential means growth collapses when it is off. Aggregated over all models this ranks which reactions most control biological flux; the models are then clustered by which reactions most influence their growth.
